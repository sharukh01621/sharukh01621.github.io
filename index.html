Ongoing Research Themes

High-level scope of active research projects
Environmental Epidemiology & Child Development

Environmental Exposures & Early Childhood Health

Investigating environmental exposure indicators and early childhood developmental outcomes using nationally representative household survey microdata, survey-weighted Bayesian modelling, and interpretable machine learning methods.

Maternal & Child Health · Complex Surveys

Health Equity, Maternal Care & Child Nutrition

Studying socioeconomic disparities, maternal healthcare utilization, birth spacing, and child growth patterns across multi-round demographic and health surveys using multilevel statistical modelling, concentration indices, and inequality decomposition.

Infectious Disease & Climate Modelling

Climate-Sensitive Vector-Borne Disease Forecasting

Exploring relationships between meteorological variability and infectious disease surveillance dynamics through lagged climate features, statistical time-series forecasting, ensemble tree models, and deep sequence architectures.

Responsible Clinical AI · Biostatistics

Fairness-Aware & Calibrated Clinical Risk Modelling

Designing probability-calibrated, subgroup-audited machine learning pipelines for chronic disease quality-of-life, mental health assessment, and explainable clinical decision support grounded in transparent reporting standards.

Field Epidemiology · Digital Surveys

Antimicrobial Resistance & Community Health Surveys

Developing quantitative survey instruments, KoboToolbox / ODK field data workflows, and mixed-methods analytical pipelines for community antimicrobial resistance awareness, oral health, and population health studies.

Open Science & Statistical Education

Reproducible Computational Education Tooling

Building open-source educational software, automated diagnostic verification libraries, and self-contained interactive cloud labs to make modern biostatistics, machine learning, and PyTorch accessible to learners globally.
